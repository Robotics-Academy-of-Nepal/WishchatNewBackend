1. Project Overview 
Dobato.net is an innovative, multi-subdomain digital platform designed to empower and promote 
local tourism, culture, and businesses throughout Nepal. By partnering exclusively with 
municipal governments, Dobato.net provides each municipality with its own dedicated and fully 
branded subdomain (e.g., kathmandu.dobato.net, pokhara.dobato.net). This localized approach 
ensures relevant, authoritative, and rich content tailored to each municipality’s unique heritage, 
attractions, services, and events. 
The platform serves as a comprehensive digital directory and interactive guide for tourists, 
residents, and businesses, offering detailed information about tourism destinations, local service 
providers, upcoming events, and municipal leadership profiles including mayors and board 
members. This centralized hub not only increases the visibility of local businesses and cultural 
sites but also encourages community engagement and sustainable tourism development. 
Key features include: 
●​ Multi-Tenant Subdomain Architecture: Each municipality has its own subdomain, 
enabling customized branding, content management, and administrative control. This 
allows municipalities to independently manage their digital presence while benefiting 
from the overall Dobato.net ecosystem. 
●​ Curated Tourism Content: Rich storytelling with historical, cultural, natural, and 
religious tourism places, supported by multimedia assets such as photos, videos, and 
audio narratives. Content is enhanced with interactive maps and embedded static map 
views, enabling users to explore destinations visually. 
●​ QR Code Integration: Unique, downloadable, and printable QR codes are generated for 
every tourism place, event, and business listing, facilitating easy physical and digital 
sharing. These QR codes link directly to the respective detailed pages on the 
municipality’s subdomain, supporting offline promotion and visitor engagement. 
●​ Text-to-Speech (TTS) Support: Story-driven content and descriptions feature bilingual 
text-to-speech capabilities in Nepali and English, allowing users with visual impairments 
or literacy challenges to access information auditorily. The system optionally highlights 
text in sync with narration for enhanced accessibility. 
●​ Interactive & Static Maps: The platform integrates both static maps (via 
OpenStreetMap or Google Maps Static API) for quick reference and fully interactive 
maps with zoom, pan, and route planning features. Interactive maps support route 
suggestions, tourist circuits, and real-time event markers to enrich the visitor experience. 
●​ Robust User Engagement: Public users can register via social logins (Google, 
Facebook, Apple ID) or mobile OTP to comment, review, and submit suggestions or new 
listings for approval by municipality admins. Personalized dashboards provide tailored 
recommendations based on user location, preferences, and behavior. 
●​ Municipality Administration Portal: Municipality admins and designated department 
managers control and curate their subdomain’s content, manage events, services, local

Role-based access controls ensure secure, department-specific management. 
●​ Comprehensive Public Profile Pages: Each municipality’s page highlights official

contact information, fostering transparency and local government visibility. 
●​ Community Feedback and Support System: Users can submit complaints,

cultural and economic vitality through technology-driven engagement and visibility. 
 
 
 
 
 
2. System Architecture & Subdomains  
Dobato.net employs a robust multi-tenant architecture designed to securely and efficiently 
serve multiple municipalities across Nepal, each with its own isolated digital environment. This 
architectural approach ensures data segregation, customized user experiences, and scalable 
management while leveraging a centralized platform infrastructure.

Key Architectural Features: 
●​ Multi-Tenant, Isolated Subdomains:​
 Each municipality operates within its own dedicated subdomain (e.g., 
kathmandu.dobato.net, lalitpur.dobato.net), ensuring complete separation of data, 
content, and user management. This isolation enhances security, governance, and 
customization specific to the local context of each municipality. 
●​ Centralized Super Admin Portal:​
 A master administrative layer is provided for platform-wide governance, operated by the 
Dobato.net core team or designated central authority. The Super Admin’s responsibilities 
include:​
 
○​ Onboarding new municipalities and provisioning their subdomains. 
○​ Managing global platform settings, compliance, and security policies. 
○​ Monitoring overall system health, usage analytics, and performance metrics. 
○​ Moderating platform-wide content or resolving escalated issues. 
○​ Managing partnerships, contracts, and MoU documentation with municipalities. 
●​ Municipality-Level Administration:​
 Each municipality has its own Admin user(s) who fully control their subdomain’s content, 
user roles, and permissions within defined boundaries. Municipality Admins can:​
 
○​ Create, update, and manage tourism places, local businesses, events, and 
stories. 
○​ Manage user roles by delegating permissions to sub-users or department 
managers. 
○​ Approve or reject content submissions from public users. 
○​ Access detailed analytics and reporting specific to their municipality. 
○​ Customize the municipality’s branding and UI elements on their subdomain.​
 
●​ Role-Based Sub-User Management:​
 Municipality Admins can create department-specific sub-users (e.g., Tourism Manager, 
Events Coordinator, Service Desk) with scoped permissions, allowing distributed content 
management and operational efficiency without compromising security or data integrity. 
These sub-users:​
 
○​ Access and manage only the content and modules assigned to their department. 
○​ Have limited or no access to user management and sensitive settings. 
○​ Operate within audit logs for transparency and accountability.​
 
●​ Scalable & Modular Backend Design:​
 The backend supports horizontal scaling to handle increasing numbers of municipalities 
and users without performance degradation. Modular APIs allow independent 
development and integration of new features such as payment gateways, third-party

●​ Wildcard DNS & SSL Support:​
 The platform leverages wildcard DNS to dynamically create and route requests to 
municipality subdomains. Each subdomain is secured with SSL/TLS certificates ensuring 
encrypted communication and user data protection.​
 
●​ Data Security & Privacy:​
 Tenant data is logically and physically isolated to comply with privacy regulations and 
safeguard sensitive municipal and user data. Access control policies enforce strict 
separation between municipalities.​
 
●​ Logging, Monitoring & Auditing:​
 Comprehensive logging of all administrative and user actions is maintained both at the 
platform and municipality levels. This supports compliance audits, troubleshooting, and 
forensic investigations if required.

3. User Roles & Permissions 
Dobato.net’s user management framework is designed to ensure secure, role-based access and 
efficient content governance, allowing different levels of control and responsibility across the 
platform. This hierarchy guarantees that users can only access features and data appropriate to 
their role, preserving data integrity and operational clarity. 
3.1 Super Admin 
●​ Holds full control and oversight of the entire Dobato.net platform. 
●​ Responsibilities include: 
○​ Creating, editing, activating, or deactivating municipalities and their associated 
admin accounts. 
○​ Assigning and managing platform-wide roles and permissions. 
○​ Overseeing content moderation across all municipalities to ensure compliance 
with platform policies and standards. 
○​ Accessing comprehensive analytics dashboards with data aggregated globally 
and filtered by municipality. 
○​ Managing legal and administrative aspects such as contracts, partnerships, and 
Memoranda of Understanding (MoU) uploads. 
○​ Monitoring system health, security logs, and user activity to detect and prevent 
abuse or breaches. 
○​ Coordinating platform-wide announcements, feature rollouts, and emergency 
communications. 
○​ Managing billing, subscription plans (if applicable), and platform resource 
allocation.

3.2 Municipality Admin 
●​ Possesses full administrative control within their own municipality subdomain but 
no access beyond it. 
●​ Key capabilities include:​
 
○​ Managing all content types related to their municipality such as tourism places, 
local businesses, events, stories, and maps. 
○​ Creating, editing, suspending, or deleting users within their municipality domain. 
○​ Assigning and delegating department-specific roles and permissions (Tourism 
Manager, Event Coordinator, Service Manager, Content Editor). 
○​ Reviewing and approving or rejecting public user submissions (listings, reviews, 
event proposals) with the option to provide feedback. 
○​ Viewing detailed analytics reports on user engagement, QR code scans, 
submission trends, and other KPIs relevant to their municipality. 
○​ Customizing the municipality’s subdomain UI elements, branding, and localized 
settings. 
○​ Enabling or disabling features such as public user submissions, social sharing, 
and notifications. 
○​ Accessing audit logs of municipality users for security and accountability. 
○​ Responding to citizen complaints, suggestions, and feedback submitted via 
dedicated forms.​
 
3.3 Municipality Department Managers / Content Editors 
●​ Operate under strictly scoped permissions tied to specific departments such as 
Tourism, Events, Business, or Services. 
●​ Department Managers:​
 
○​ Have full rights to create, edit, and delete content within their department’s 
domain. 
○​ Manage daily operations of their department’s listings, events, and service 
entries. 
○​ Moderate user-generated content (comments, reviews, media uploads) relevant 
to their department. 
○​ Cannot manage users or assign roles but can flag critical issues to Municipality 
Admins.​
 
●​ Content Editors:​
 
○​ Have limited editing privileges restricted to updating existing content without 
delete rights. 
○​ Cannot approve or publish new content without oversight.

○​ Do not have access to user management or sensitive administrative features. 
●​ All department-level actions are tracked in audit logs for transparency and governance.​
 
3.4 Public Users 
●​ General visitors and registered users with tiered engagement levels: 
○​ Anonymous visitors: Can browse all public content without logging in, including 
tourism places, events, businesses, maps, and municipal info. 
○​ Registered users:​
 
■​ Can sign up and log in via secure social login providers (Google, 
Facebook, Apple ID) or via mobile OTP verification for easy access. 
■​ Gain the ability to post comments and reviews on tourism places, 
businesses, and events to foster community feedback. 
■​ Submit new listings, business profiles, or event suggestions for review 
and approval by municipal admins, contributing to platform growth and 
accuracy. 
■​ Save favorites/bookmarks and manage a personalized list of preferred 
places and events (feature planned for near-future rollout). 
■​ Receive personalized content recommendations and interactive map 
suggestions based on their geolocation or manual municipality selection, 
improving relevance and user experience. 
■​ Manage notification preferences for event alerts, submission status 
updates, and municipal announcements.​
 
●​ User data is securely stored with privacy and consent management aligned with 
applicable regulations.​
