**Abroad Unbox: Transforming Dreams into Reality**

### About Us

Abroad Unbox is a premier education consultancy based in Nepal, dedicated to helping students achieve their dreams of studying in the United States. With a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities that come with studying abroad, we provide comprehensive guidance and support at every step of the journey. Our expert consultants, with years of experience in international education, ensure that students find the right institutions, secure scholarships, and navigate the visa process seamlessly.

### Our Vision

At Abroad Unbox, our vision is to empower Nepali students with world-class education opportunities by connecting them with the best universities in the United States. We believe that education is the foundation of success, and we are committed to guiding students toward a brighter future by providing them with the right resources, mentorship, and support.

### Services We Offer

1. **University Selection & Application Assistance**

   - Personalized counseling to match students with the best-fit universities.
   - Assistance with application forms, essays, and recommendation letters.

2. **Test Preparation & Language Proficiency Guidance**

   - Training for standardized tests such as SAT, GRE, GMAT, TOEFL, and IELTS.

3. **Visa Application Support**

   - Step-by-step guidance through the visa application process.
   - Mock visa interviews to build confidence and ensure success.

4. **Scholarship & Financial Aid Assistance**

   - Help in identifying and applying for scholarships and financial aid opportunities.

5. **Pre-Departure & Post-Arrival Support**

   - Orientation sessions for students before departure.
   - Assistance with accommodation, travel, and settling into university life.
